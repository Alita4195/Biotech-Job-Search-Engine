# High-Impact Preference Questions

A resume tells the engine what you *can* do. These answers tell it what you *want* to do.

If ChatGPT cannot infer these reliably, answer them before your first run:

1. What seniority levels are you targeting? Which are definitely too junior or too senior?
2. Do you want individual-contributor roles, people-management roles, or both?
3. Which job families should be actively promoted? Which should be excluded even when technically relevant?
4. What scientific/application areas do you most want to continue working in?
5. What geographic locations are acceptable? Is remote required/preferred? How much travel is acceptable?
6. Is there a compensation floor or preferred range that should influence review decisions?
7. Are there industries/environments you prefer or want to avoid (diagnostics, pharma, sequencing platforms, academia/contracting, etc.)?
8. Are customer-facing/application roles attractive, or do you want primarily internal R&D?
9. Are regulated/clinical responsibilities desirable, neutral, or a mismatch?
10. Name 2–5 past postings that felt like excellent fits, if available.

Do not turn every answer into a hard filter. Hard filters are appropriate only for genuine constraints; other preferences are usually better represented as score weights or penalties.
